Cheap Eats in Krakow: Eating Well Under 30 Zloty

By Cezary Musiał · Published 27 August 2026 · Prices checked August 2026

Krakow is one of the best-value food cities in Europe, if you know where to point. Skip the Main Square terraces, where a mediocre plate of pierogi costs what a great one does two streets away, and you can eat three proper meals a day for less than a single tourist dinner. This is the budget map: milk bars, hand-folded pierogi, the best kebab in town and a curry from sixteen zloty, all real, all open, all cheap.

Old Town · milk bar

Bar mleczny Pod Temidą

A surviving communist-era milk bar on Grodzka, halfway between the Main Square and Wawel, charging under twenty zloty for most plates in prime tourist territory. Order at the counter, share a table, and eat pierogi ruskie, żurek and potato pancakes the way the city has for decades. More in our Polish and pierogi guide.

How to eat Krakow on a budget

Lunch at a milk bar or U Stasi, grab a banh mi or a market taco between sights, and make dinner a kebab at Jamra or a curry at Zayka. Add the falafel and hummus spots in our Middle Eastern guide and the fruit pierogi at the pierogi classics, and you can eat brilliantly all day for the price of one Main Square dinner. Questions budget travelers ask

What is the cheapest good meal in Krakow?

A milk bar plate at Pod Temidą, under twenty zloty for pierogi or żurek. For the best value overall, a kebab at Jamra or a curry at Zayka from sixteen zloty is hard to beat.

Can I eat cheaply and still eat well?

Absolutely, and that is the point of this list. Every place here is somewhere locals actually eat, not a compromise. The best pierogi and the best kebab in the city are also among the cheapest.

Where do students eat cheaply?

The same places, plus the cafes in our student cafes guide for a coffee and a long sit. The banh mi window and the milk bars are student staples.
