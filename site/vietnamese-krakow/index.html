The Best Vietnamese Food in Krakow

By Cezary Musiał · Published 4 August 2026 · Addresses and prices checked August 2026

Poland has one of the largest Vietnamese communities in Europe, and Krakow eats very well because of it. This is the best-value cuisine in the city: a bowl of pho that took sixteen hours to make for the price of a coffee and cake, family kitchens where the owner's mother is at the stove, and banh mi that would hold its own in Hanoi. Nobody is charging fine-dining prices, and nobody needs to.

So a framing before the list: there is no expensive Vietnamese in Krakow, and that is the whole point. The top end here means a comfortable sit-down room at forty to sixty zloty a head, and the value end is a genuine hole-in-the-wall doing one thing perfectly. Six places, from the best pho to the best sandwich.

Kazimierz · pho

Pho Ever

The pho benchmark, on Dajwór in Kazimierz. The beef broth is simmered with bones for sixteen hours and built with medicinal herbs, and the noodles and dumplings are made by hand in house. It was founded by a Vietnamese restaurateur who runs restaurants in Vietnam and Bali, and it has the highest review volume of any Vietnamese spot in the city, roughly five thousand of them. Start here.

ul. Dajwór 25c, Kazimierz Daily 11:30 to 21:30 $$
9.5/10
What to order
Pho bo + handmade dumplings + fresh spring rolls

A sixteen-hour beef-bone broth and noodles made by hand, from a Vietnamese founder who cooks across three countries. The most-reviewed Vietnamese in Krakow, and the pho to measure the others against.

Old Town · pho

Bonjour Pho

The best-known polished pho house in the city, just off Krupnicza near the Old Town, and the most convenient authentic bowl if you are staying in the center. It opened around 2019 on the Asian-food wave and has stayed a consensus top pick, with reviewers singling out the long-simmered beef broth and generous portions. The slow-cooked brisket pho is the one to order.

ul. Krupnicza 12, Old Town Daily from 12:00, later Wed to Sat $$
9/10

Slow-cooked brisket pho + bun cha

The most convenient authentic pho in the center, with a long-simmered broth and portions that reviewers keep calling generous. The safe central choice.

Podgórze · family

Wietnam

One of the earliest Vietnamese restaurants in Krakow and still one of the most genuine, on Brodzińskiego in Podgorze. It is properly family-run, with the owner's mother as head chef cooking home-style Vietnamese, and it anchors a little Vietnamese cluster on the street. This is the bowl to order when you want the food someone's family actually eats, not a restaurant version of it.

ul. Kazimierza Brodzińskiego 2, Podgórze Lunch and dinner $$
9/10
What to order
Family-recipe pho bo + fresh spring rolls

Family-run with the mother at the stove, one of the first Vietnamese kitchens in the city and still one of the most home-style. Cross the river for it.

Kazimierz · theatre

Yum Yum Viet Nam

Right on Józefa, the main restaurant street of Kazimierz, a spacious, welcoming room with a nice bit of theatre: the pho is poured tableside over rare beef, cooked in front of you when the hot broth hits the bowl, which is the traditional way to serve pho tai. Fresh spring rolls, grilled pork, Vietnamese iced coffee, and vegan options too. The easy, central Kazimierz choice.

ul. Józefa 20, Kazimierz Daily, late on Fri and Sat $$
8.5/10
What to order
Pho tai poured over raw beef at the table + iced coffee

Tableside broth over rare beef, the traditional pho tai served with a bit of show, on the main Kazimierz restaurant street. Spacious, easy, good vegan options.

Krowodrza · banh mi

Banh Mi Dzień Dobry

The best banh mi in Krakow, full stop, from a tiny Vietnamese-couple-run street-food window near AGH University. Seven varieties, each with pate and house-made mayo, plus spring rolls and pho if you want more. This is the grab-and-go sandwich end of the cuisine done exactly right, for under twenty-five zloty. Two catches for the guide: it is lunch-focused and it closes at weekends, so it is a weekday plan.

ul. Mazowiecka 26B, Krowodrza Mon to Fri, lunch focused, closed weekends $

A tiny couple-run window doing the best banh mi in the city, under twenty-five zloty. The sandwich is the whole point. Weekday lunch only, so plan around it.

Kleparz · by the station

Tre Viet

A small bistro on Szlak in Kleparz, a short walk from the main station and Galeria Krakowska, with the broadest budget menu of the group: cheap banh mi, pho including a tofu-and-veg version, bun bo noodle salad, bubble tea. It is the reliable, no-frills first or last meal in the city, steps from the platforms. Not the most refined pho, but the most convenient one for arrivals and departures.

How to use this list

For the best pho, Pho Ever in Kazimierz or Bonjour Pho in the center. For the most home-style bowl, cross to Wietnam in Podgorze. For a sandwich, Banh Mi Dzień Dobry near AGH on a weekday, and for something quick by the station, Tre Viet. Yum Yum is the easy Kazimierz sit-down with tableside theatre.

Two honorable mentions if you want to go deeper: BaWietka, the Wietnam family's banh mi bar on Mostowa in Kazimierz, does a beef pho braised with red wine, and Przystanek Wietnam in Prądnik Biały is worth the trip north for banh mi and Vietnamese coffee. And one thing to know: MoLam appears on some Vietnamese lists but is actually Thai, and holds the city's only Asian Michelin Bib Gourmand, so we will cover it under Thai.

Questions people actually ask

Is the pho spicy?

No, not by default. Vietnamese food here is aromatic rather than fiery. The bowl arrives mild and the heat lives on the side: fresh chili, sriracha, chili oil. Add it to taste, or ask for it ostro if you want the kitchen to bring it hot.

Where is the best banh mi in Krakow?

Banh Mi Dzień Dobry on Mazowiecka near AGH University, run by a Vietnamese couple, seven varieties with pate and house mayo, under twenty-five zloty. It is lunch-only and closed at weekends, so it is a weekday plan.

Is Vietnamese good for vegetarians and vegans?

Yes. Fresh spring rolls, tofu-and-vegetable pho and noodle salads are on most menus. Yum Yum and Tre Viet both flag vegan and vegetarian options clearly, and the herbs-and-broth style suits plant-based eating well.
