7 Best Outdoor Cafes in Krakow

By Cezary Musiał · Published 15 August 2026 · All gardens visited summer 2026, details checked August 2026

Krakow has a short summer and knows it. From May to September the city pushes every table, chair and bench it can find onto sidewalks, into courtyards and through medieval archways. Some of these outdoor spaces are afterthoughts: a plastic chair next to a busy road. Others are the entire point of visiting. This list covers the second kind.

Seven spots across four neighborhoods, from a secret Art Nouveau garden the guidebooks have not found yet to the biggest beer garden in the city. All tested with an actual drink in hand, not from a Google Street View car. One rule: if the inside is nicer than the outside, it did not make the list.

01

Meho Cafe

Behind the Jozef Mehoffer House on Krupnicza, through an unmarked gate, is what might be the best-kept secret in Krakow's cafe scene. An Art Nouveau garden full of roses, fruit trees and birdsong, maintained by the National Museum but used almost exclusively by people who happen to know it exists. The cafe itself is tiny and the menu is simple: coffee, lemonade, cheesecake. Nobody comes for the menu. They come because this garden feels stolen from 19th-century Paris and dropped behind a Krakow townhouse. Most of the seating is outside, which tells you where the priorities are.

ul. Krupnicza 26 Daily 10:00 to 21:00, garden April to October $
8/10
What I ordered
Iced lemonade + cheesecake

The cheesecake is good. The garden is extraordinary. That ratio tells you everything about why this place works.

02

Mleczarnia

The courtyard at Mleczarnia is one of those places that photographs better than it has any right to. String lights, mismatched vintage furniture, old armchairs, ivy climbing the walls. Scenes from Schindler's List were filmed here, though the regulars on a Friday evening are more interested in the craft beer list than the film history. It sits at the corner of Plac Nowy in Kazimierz, so the location alone would make it busy, but the courtyard is the reason people stay. Come early on a weekend or expect to wait.

ul. Meiselsa 20, Kazimierz Daily 10:00 to 23:00, Fri and Sat until midnight $
7.5/10
What I ordered
Craft IPA + homemade lemonade in the courtyard

The drinks are honest, the pastries are decent, but the courtyard is doing ninety percent of the work here and it knows it.

03

Kolanko No 6

A large inner courtyard behind a former hydraulic company, Kolanko has been running since 2002 and knows exactly what it is doing with outdoor space. Mature trees provide shade all day, the tables are spaced far enough apart that you forget you are in the center of a city, and in winter the garden is heated so it operates year-round. The menu is ambitious in the international direction: Thai curry, Greek moussaka, Indian butter chicken, Polish crepes. Not all of it lands equally, but the garden compensates for any kitchen inconsistency and the breakfast buffet on weekends is a safe bet.

ul. Józefa 17, Kazimierz Lunch and dinner, garden heated year-round $$
8/10
What I ordered
Weekend breakfast buffet + flat white under the trees

The buffet is generous and the setting makes it. Sitting under mature trees in a heated courtyard in January while it snows outside is one of the better tricks in the city.

04

Stara Zajezdnia

When Krakow wants a proper beer garden, it goes to a converted 19th-century tram depot in Kazimierz. Stara Zajezdnia brews its own beer on-site, including a pear wheat, a plum dark, and a honey lager that are all better than they have any right to be from a venue this size. The garden is enormous: hundreds of deck chairs and beach chairs fill the space when the sun comes out, and on summer weekends the place runs outdoor grills and live cooking stations. The industrial heritage architecture, original tram shed beams still visible overhead, gives the whole thing a scale that no courtyard in the city can match.

ul. Św. Wawrzyńca 12, Kazimierz Mon to Fri from 15:00, weekends from 12:00 $$
8/10
What I ordered
Tasting set of four house beers + burger

The pear wheat is the surprise. The burger is solid. The setting, a beer garden inside a tram depot, is the thing you tell people about.

05

Trattoria La Campana

Kanonicza is one of the oldest streets in Krakow, a cobbled corridor between the Main Square and Wawel Castle that most people walk down without looking sideways. Push through the archway at number 7 and you find a mature, ivy-covered garden hidden at the rear of a medieval townhouse. Tall trees, tables spaced far enough apart for a conversation that stays private, and in summer, light filtering through the leaves in a way that makes you forget you are five minutes from the busiest tourist corridor in Poland. The food is straightforward Italian trattoria: pastas, meats, a wine list that takes its job seriously. Book for a garden table on weekends or you will watch from the vaulted cellar inside, which is beautiful in its own right but not why you came.

ul. Kanonicza 7, Old Town Lunch and dinner, garden May to September $$-$$$
8.5/10
What I ordered
Bruschetta + tagliatelle with porcini + glass of Montepulciano

Clean flavors, nothing over-complicated. The garden turns a good meal into an excellent evening. This is how outdoor dining should feel.

06

Drukarnia

The best riverside terrace in the city sits on the Podgorze embankment, directly on the Vistula, facing the Kazimierz rooftops and the distant spires of the Old Town. A former printing house turned cocktail bar and jazz club, Drukarnia has been the default sunset spot for years and the terrace is why. The craft cocktails are well-made, the tapas menu covers enough ground, and the brunch on weekends draws a crowd. After dark the basement hosts live jazz, which makes it one of the few places on this list where you might arrive for a terrace drink at six and leave at midnight.

ul. Nadwiślańska 1, Podgorze Daily from 9:00, Fri to Sat until 4:00 $$
8/10
What I ordered
Aperol Spritz + hummus plate on the terrace at sunset

The cocktails are good, the food is fine, and the Vistula does the rest. Come for golden hour and stay for the jazz downstairs.

07

Baqaro

At the edge of Stary Kleparz, one of the oldest open-air markets in Krakow, Baqaro sets tables among the market stalls and serves Italian aperitivo as if someone transplanted a fragment of Venice into a Polish market square. The Aperol Spritz is made properly. The tramezzini sandwiches are filled generously. The Gillardeau oysters are an unexpected luxury in a setting this casual. On a warm evening, sitting with a spritz among the market stalls while the last flower vendors pack up for the day, you understand why the concept works despite having no right to. Five minutes from the Kleparz guide's other picks, and a very different energy from all of them.

Rynek Kleparski 20/41, Kleparz Outdoor seating spring to autumn $$
8.5/10
What I ordered
Aperol Spritz + prosciutto tramezzini + Ichnusa beer

The Spritz is correct. The tramezzini are generous. The Ichnusa is the one Sardinian beer you cannot find anywhere else in Krakow. Small victories.

The seasonal map

Peak terrace season runs from late May through September. April and October are unpredictable: some gardens open, others wait for warmer days. Kolanko No 6 is the exception, heated year-round, so if you are reading this in winter that is your answer. In summer, a good route covers three neighborhoods in one evening: start with coffee or lemonade at Meho Cafe, walk through Kazimierz for an early drink at Mleczarnia or beers at Stara Zajezdnia, then cross the river to Drukarnia for sunset. Baqaro at the Kleparz market is better as a standalone afternoon aperitivo, and La Campana near Wawel deserves its own dinner booking.

One name you might look for on older lists: Forum Przestrzenie, the brutalist riverside bar with beach chairs and Wawel views. It closed in March 2024 and is not coming back. The spots above have filled the gap.

Questions people actually ask me

When is outdoor cafe season in Krakow?

Most gardens and terraces open in late April or early May and close by mid-October. Peak is June through September. A few spots like Kolanko No 6 heat their gardens and stay open year-round. Pack a layer for the shoulder months.

Do I need to book for a garden table?

At La Campana and Kolanko No 6, yes, especially on summer weekends and evenings. Mleczarnia and Stara Zajezdnia are large enough that walk-ins rarely have trouble. Weekday lunches anywhere are usually fine without a reservation.

Are outdoor cafes in Krakow expensive?

Good value compared to Western Europe. A coffee runs 12 to 20 PLN (3 to 5 EUR), a craft beer 15 to 25 PLN, and a full meal at a terrace restaurant 40 to 80 PLN per person. Market-side Baqaro and the Stara Zajezdnia beer garden offer the best value. La Campana costs more but still undercuts its equivalents in Prague or Vienna.

Which neighborhood has the best outdoor dining?

Kazimierz has the highest concentration: Mleczarnia, Kolanko No 6, and Stara Zajezdnia are all within a ten-minute walk. Podgorze has the best waterfront. The Old Town hides a few gems behind medieval facades. And Kleparz has Baqaro at the market. Our Kazimierz guide and Podgorze guide cover each area in full.
