Krakow Nightlife: Where to Go Out, and Who Is Still Open

By Cezary Musiał · Published 27 October 2026 · Hours checked October 2026 · How we test

Krakow goes out late and goes out underground. Half the good bars are in brick cellars under the Old Town or in the old Jewish quarter of Kazimierz, there is no fixed closing time, and a normal Friday ends somewhere between 2:00 and the first tram. The trick is knowing which room fits the night you want, and which ones are still open when you want the next one.

So this guide does two things. It names the bars we have reviewed, with what to order and our score, and it lists every place here with its Friday and Saturday closing time, marked live with what is open right now. Places we have not reviewed yet are labelled that way, with facts from their Google listings and no verdict.

One good night, in order

A glass of Polish wine at Moti, cocktails at Mercy Brown, Kazimierz after midnight, zapiekanka on Plac Nowy on the way home.

Start early in Kleparz, walk ten minutes to Mercy Brown off the Planty, then cross to Kazimierz, where Singer and Alchemia are still going long after the cocktail bars close. Endzior on Plac Nowy serves zapiekanka until 2:00.

Where to go, by district

Kazimierz is the answer for most nights: Plac Nowy, Estery, Józefa and Szeroka have more bars per street than anywhere else in the city, from Singer and Alchemia to the Hevre prayer house, CiutCiut for wine and William Rabbit's hidden door. It also has the latest food, at the zapiekanka windows on Plac Nowy.

Old Town means cellars: jazz under the Main Square at Harris and on Floriańska at u Muniaka, cocktails at Mercy Brown, Mr. Black and Movida, craft beer on Szewska and św. Tomasza, and the late clubs. Floriańska and the streets right off the Square are also where the promoters and stag groups gather, so walk one street further.

Podgórze and Zabłocie, across the river, are calmer: Drukarnia on the waterfront with jazz in the basement, and Krakó Slow Wines for natural wine. Kleparz, by the main station, is the quiet start: Moti on the market square pours Polish wine until midnight.

Food after midnight

The classic is a zapiekanka from the round hall on Plac Nowy, where Endzior serves until 2:00 every night. The grilled kiełbasa from the blue van by Hala Targowa runs in the evenings, usually Monday to Saturday. More in our Polish food guide.

The rules, and getting home

Shops stop selling alcohol at midnight. Since July 2023 shops and petrol stations across Krakow cannot sell alcohol from midnight to 5:30. Bars and restaurants are not affected.

No drinking in the street. Open alcohol in streets, squares and parks is illegal and fined, Planty included. Smoking is banned inside bars, and you need to be 18 to buy a drink, so carry ID.

Watch for the club trap. The US Embassy in Poland has warned about drink spiking and inflated card bills at some clubs. Ignore promoters and friendly strangers inviting you somewhere near the Main Square, ask for prices before you order, and keep your card in sight.

Getting home. Night trams 62, 64 and 69 and night buses numbered 6xx run through the night, and Uber and Bolt work across the city. Skip the unmarked taxis waiting outside clubs. Tipping is around ten percent at table service, nothing at the bar.

Questions people actually ask

Where is the best area for nightlife in Krakow?

Kazimierz, around Plac Nowy, Estery, Józefa and Szeroka, has the densest mix of bars and stays busy latest. The Old Town has the cellar bars, jazz clubs and big clubs within a few streets of the Main Square. For a quieter night, Kleparz by the main station and Zabłocie across the river have the wine bars.

What time do bars close in Krakow?

There is no fixed closing time. Most cocktail and wine bars close between midnight and 2:00, later on Friday and Saturday. Some bars run much longer: Singer in Kazimierz until 6:00 and Pijalnia Wódki i Piwa on Szewska until 5:00 at weekends, and the club Prozak 2.0 until 8:00, according to their Google listings.

Can you buy alcohol at night in Krakow?

Not in shops. Since July 2023 shops and petrol stations across Krakow cannot sell alcohol between midnight and 5:30. Bars, pubs and restaurants are not affected. Drinking in the street or in parks is illegal and fined.

Is Krakow nightlife safe?

Generally yes, with one known trap. The US Embassy in Poland has warned about drink spiking and inflated card bills at some clubs. Ignore street promoters and friendly strangers inviting you to a bar near the Main Square, check prices before you order, and keep your card in sight.

How do I get home at night in Krakow?

Night trams 62, 64 and 69 and night buses numbered 6xx run through the night, and ride-hailing apps such as Uber and Bolt work across the city. Avoid unmarked taxis waiting outside clubs.

What is the best cocktail bar in Krakow?

Our top score goes to Mercy Brown, off the Planty on Straszewskiego: house ferments, live music most weeks, open until 1:00 at weekends. For later drinks, Mr. Black on Szewska stays open until 2:30 on Friday and Saturday.

Are bars open on Monday in Krakow?

Most are, but check first. Mercy Brown and Krakó Slow Wines close on Mondays, while Mr. Black, Movida, William Rabbit, CiutCiut, Moti and Hevre all open every day. The live table on this page shows who is open right now.
