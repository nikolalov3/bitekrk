The 10 Best Restaurants in Krakow

By Cezary Musiał · Published 26 August 2026 · Addresses, prices and Michelin status checked August 2026

Krakow punches far above a city its size. It has Poland's only two-Michelin-star kitchen, a Neapolitan pizza scene that would hold up in Naples, and Georgian, Vietnamese, Thai and Indian cooking as good as anything the Old Town serves in Polish. This is our ranking of the ten best restaurants in the city, one standout per kitchen so the list actually helps you choose, from the tasting-menu apex to a kebab worth crossing the river for. Every place is open, visited and current.

A note on the order: it is not a claim that pizza beats sushi. It is our read on which tables deliver the most, for what they are, right now. For the full field in any category, each entry links to the cuisine guide behind it.

01 · Modern Polish · two Michelin stars

Bottiglieria 1881

The best restaurant in Poland by the only ranking that counts stars. In an 1881 wine warehouse on quiet Bocheńska, chef Przemysław Klima cooks refined modern Polish as a tasting menu only, above a 1,600-bottle cellar. Two Michelin stars in the 2026 guide, the first double in the country's history. Book weeks ahead. More traditional Polish is in our Polish and pierogi guide.

How to plan a trip around this list

Base yourself in Kazimierz, where five of these ten are within a ten-minute walk. Book Bottiglieria 1881 the moment you have dates. Keep one night for traditional Polish at Pod Baranem, one for the pizza at Nolio, and let the rest be Georgian, Vietnamese and Thai, which is where Krakow surprises people. Questions visitors actually ask

What is the single best restaurant in Krakow?

Bottiglieria 1881, by the only measure that counts stars: two in the 2026 Michelin Guide, the first double star in Poland. It is a tasting-menu-only modern Polish kitchen and books out weeks ahead.

Where do locals eat, not just tourists?

Every place on this list is somewhere Krakow residents actually go. The tourist traps cluster on the Main Square; the best kitchens are a few minutes out, in Kazimierz, Zabłocie and off the Rynek. Follow the queues of locals, not the menus in five languages.

Do I need to book?

Bottiglieria 1881, weeks ahead. Hana Sushi, Hankki and Tbilisuri, worth booking for weekend dinners. Nolio and Jamra are walk-ins where you may queue. The rest usually take walk-ins outside peak hours.
