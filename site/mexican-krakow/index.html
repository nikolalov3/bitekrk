The Best Mexican Food in Krakow

By Cezary Musiał · Published 15 August 2026 · Addresses and prices checked August 2026

Mexican in Krakow splits cleanly in two, and knowing which half you are ordering from is the whole game. The authentic half is Mexican-run and lives outside the tourist core: a Yucatán family in Podgorze, a birria specialist in Zabłocie, and a native-Mexican market stall pressing its own tortillas at Nowy Kleparz. The other half is the Kazimierz cantina scene, Tex-Mex leaning, which happens to include the best margaritas in the city. This list covers both, labeled honestly.

One correction to the map before we start: several of these places moved or renamed recently, and aggregator sites have not caught up. The addresses below are the current ones, checked this month.

Podgórze · the real thing

Tacoteka

Run by a Mexican family with Yucatán roots, on Kalwaryjska in Podgorze, and the first answer to "where is the actual Mexican food". Tacos al pastor, birria tacos and a Quesadilla Yucatán that nods to home. A reviewer from Mexico called the birria the most authentic they had eaten outside Mexico, which is the kind of sentence marketing cannot buy. It opened under the name Alebriche, so older listings may confuse you.

ul. Kalwaryjska 75, Podgórze Daily 12:00 to 21:00 $$
9.5/10
What to order
Tacos al pastor + birria tacos + Quesadilla Yucatán

A Yucatán family cooking their own food, and a Mexican reviewer calling the birria the most authentic outside Mexico. The realest plate of this cuisine in the city.

Zabłocie · birria

Del Jero Mexican Street Food

The birria specialist, now on Lipowa in Zabłocie near the Schindler museum block after moving from Starowiślna, with a Google rating around 4.8 across roughly a thousand reviews. The birria tacos are the order, dipped and dripping, with the quesadilla gringa, al pastor pork with grilled pineapple, as the co-headliner. Chilaquiles for anyone chasing breakfast flavors after a museum morning.

ul. Lipowa 4D, Zabłocie Mon to Sat 12:00 to 21:00, Sun from 12:30 $
9/10
What to order
Birria tacos + quesadilla gringa with grilled pineapple

The birria destination, around 4.8 from a thousand reviews, two minutes from MOCAK. Order the gringa too and argue about which is better on the walk back.

Kleparz · market stall

Mexican Food, Nowy Kleparz

A stall inside the Nowy Kleparz market with a native Mexican owner, tortillas made in-house, and one of the only torta menus in Krakow, the Mexican sandwich the city otherwise does not know it is missing. Chicken tacos, nachos with the house salsas, and market prices. The name is generic, the cooking is not.

Plac Targowy Nowy Kleparz 18, Kleparz Daily 11:00 to 22:00 $
8.5/10
What to order
Torta + chicken tacos with house salsas

A native Mexican pressing tortillas inside a Krakow market, serving the torta almost nobody else in the city makes. The kind of stall food cities get bragging rights for.

Kazimierz · margaritas

Manzana 3.0

The third incarnation of a Kazimierz institution, now on Kupa after years on Miodowa, and the reason to come is the bar: the largest tequila range in Krakow and the margaritas locals cite as the city's best, led by the jalapeño pour. The food is cantina Tex-Mex done properly, beef fajitas on a hot plate, chicken enchilada. Come for a loud dinner that turns into a longer night.

ul. Kupa 6, Kazimierz Daily, lunch and dinner $$$
8.5/10
What to order
Jalapeño margarita + beef fajitas on the hot plate

The biggest tequila wall in the city and the margaritas everyone measures against. The food is honest cantina Tex-Mex, and with that drink in hand, exactly right.

Plac Nowy · in between

Puente

On Plac Nowy, Puente lands between the camps: a kitchen cooking with imported Mexican chiles and products, everything made on site, sopes and tacos with real character, and weekday lunch sets under forty zloty. Live-music evenings spill onto the square. The name means bridge, which is roughly what it is between the taquerias and the cantinas.

Plac Nowy 3, Kazimierz Weekdays from 17:00, weekends from 12:00 $$
8.5/10
What to order
Sopes + tacos, or the weekday lunch set

Imported Mexican chiles, everything made on site, and a lunch set under forty zloty on Plac Nowy. The middle ground between stall and cantina, done with care.

Kazimierz · counter

Los Gorditos

Counter-service Tex-Mex on Augustiańska that earns its spot with the details: tortilla chips fried in-house, salsas made fresh, and a burrito bowl that makes a fast dinner honest. The same team runs a breakfast offshoot at plac Wolnica, which tells you they like mornings too. The easy, cheap, no-booking option in Kazimierz.

ul. Augustiańska 24, Kazimierz Sun to Thu 12:00 to 21:00, Fri and Sat to 22:00 $$
8/10
What to order
Fresh totopos with salsas + burrito bowl

Chips fried in-house and salsas made fresh, which most counter spots skip. The reliable quick Mexican in Kazimierz when you want food, not an evening.

How to use this list

For the real thing, cross the river: Tacoteka on Kalwaryjska and Del Jero in Zabłocie pair perfectly with a Podgorze afternoon. For tortas and tortillas pressed by hand, hit the Nowy Kleparz stall after the Kleparz market. And for a margarita night in Kazimierz, book Manzana 3.0 and let the tequila wall make the decisions.

Questions people actually ask

What is birria and why does everyone order it?

Slow-stewed beef, served in tacos you dip into the cooking broth. It is the dish that took over street food worldwide, and Del Jero and Tacoteka make the two versions worth arguing about in Krakow.

Which places are genuinely Mexican-run?

Tacoteka (Yucatán family), Del Jero, and the Mexican Food stall at Nowy Kleparz (native Mexican owner). The Kazimierz spots are Polish-run and lean Tex-Mex, which they do not hide.

Moved or renamed places I should know about?

Manzana is now Manzana 3.0 at Kupa 6, not Miodowa. Del Jero moved from Starowiślna to Lipowa 4D in Zabłocie. Tacoteka opened as Alebriche. Older listings still show all three wrong.
