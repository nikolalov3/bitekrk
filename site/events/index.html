Wine fair (trade) · EXPO Kraków

ENOEXPO international wine fair

4 to 6 November 2026

Poland's big international wine fair, held together with the HORECA show: tastings, master classes and the Polish Wine and Cider Competition results. Mostly for importers and restaurants, so confirm visitor access before you go.

Christmas market · Rynek Główny, the Main Market Square

Christmas market on the Main Square

Expected late November 2026 to 1 January 2027

Grilled oscypek, kiełbasa, pierogi, bigos and mulled wine from wooden stalls on the Main Square. The organiser has not announced 2026 dates yet; last year it ran from 28 November to 1 January.
