The Best Kebab and Middle Eastern Food in Krakow

By Cezary Musiał · Published 20 August 2026 · Addresses and prices checked August 2026

Krakow has over a hundred kebab points and about a dozen that matter, the ones roasting house-marinated meat on their own spits. Those dozen are what this city actually argues about at one in the morning, and the argument has real stakes: Syrian charcoal grills, a doner with the biggest top-rated review base in town, and, since last autumn, a place baking every flatbread to order in a real tandoor. Around them sits the wider Levantine scene, Israeli mezze on Szeroka and a hummus bar with twenty-five varieties.

One update for anyone using older lists: Sham Falafel, long the go-to Syrian vegan stall, shows every sign of being gone, its site offline and delivery listings closed. The seven below were all verified open this month.

Zabłocie · the champion

Jamra

A Syrian-run halal grill in Zabłocie, open since early 2020, and the name local video reviewers keep concluding with: the best kebab in Krakow. The dish to understand why is the Aleppo wrap, charcoal-grilled meat with parsley and the house garlic paste that people order extra pots of. The Haskurije with jalapeño is the spicy answer, the mixed grill plate the full argument. A second branch runs on Legionów Piłsudskiego.

ul. Zabłocie 25A, Zabłocie Daily, roughly 11:00 to midnight $
9.5/10

Syrian charcoal grilling and a garlic paste with its own fan base. The kebab the rest of the city gets measured against, and usually loses to.

Kleparz · the crowd's pick

Kebab Soltan

On Warszawska near the Politechnika, Soltan holds roughly 4.8 stars across more than 2,300 Google reviews, the biggest top-rated review base of any kebab in the city. Halal beef doner, the house signature sauces, and hours that answer the real question: open until about two in the morning on Friday and Saturday nights, when the argument about the best kebab gets loudest and hungriest.

ul. Warszawska 14, Kleparz Daily to midnight, to ~2:00 Fri and Sat $
9/10
What to order
Beef doner in bread with the signature sauces

4.8 stars from over 2,300 reviews and open past midnight on weekends. The consistency champion, and the late-night answer near the station.

Kazimierz · fresh from the tandoor

Tandoor Kebab

The newcomer changing the format. Opened autumn 2025 at the river end of Starowiślna, it bakes each flatbread to order in a real tandoor oven, reportedly the first kebab spot in Krakow to do so, and the difference is the first thing you taste. Local bloggers rate the oriental-spiced chicken over the fattier beef. Halal, around 4.7 from over a thousand reviews in its first year.

ul. Starowiślna 97, Kazimierz Lunch and dinner daily $
9/10
What to order
Spiced chicken kebab in tandoor-baked flatbread

Every flatbread baked to order in a real tandoor, which no other kebab in the city does. Order the chicken, watch the bread happen, recalibrate your standards.

Stary Kleparz · the counterpick

Arabia Kebab

The food-blogger counterpick to Jamra in the best-in-Krakow argument, a stall at the Stary Kleparz market holding around 4.9 on Tripadvisor after a recent interior upgrade. Syrian shawarma is the order, the mixed sampler plate the tour, and reviewers keep mentioning the same detail: the owner hands first-timers a complimentary sweet tea and baklava, which is hospitality as marketing and works completely.

Rynek Kleparski 5, Kleparz Lunch and dinner $
9/10
What to order
Syrian shawarma + the mixed sampler plate

The other half of the city's best-kebab argument, at the old market, with sweet tea and baklava on the house for first-timers. Come hungry and undecided.

Kazimierz · mezze

Hamsa

On the Szeroka square in the heart of Jewish Kazimierz, Hamsa has been serving Israeli mezze since 2012, opened by three Polish friends who fell for the food culture, one of whom had waited tables in a Jewish restaurant at this very address. Hummus with slow-cooked beef, a mezze spread for the table, shakshuka, and a wide vegan and gluten-free range. Weekend breakfast from nine makes it the morning option here too.

Kazimierz · hummus

Hummus Amamamusi

A fully vegetarian hummus specialist on Meiselsa running about twenty-five varieties, made fresh on site every morning, with a rotating seasonal special that has included pumpkin and rhubarb. The classic plate with pita is the benchmark order. Two planning notes: it closes at eight in the evening and is shut on Sundays, because fresh-every-morning cuts both ways.

8.5/10

Piasek · falafel

Mazaya

The Syrian falafel kitchen that grew into a small Krakow institution, four locations and counting, with the Krupnicza flagship opening at eight in the morning for a Levantine breakfast that includes shakshuka. The falafel sandwich is the order that built the name, hummus with sweet mint tea the way to stay longer. The cheapest sit-down Levantine meal in the center.

ul. Krupnicza 22, Piasek Daily 11:00 to 22:00, Krupnicza from 8:00 for breakfast $
8.5/10

Syrian falafel sandwich + hummus with mint tea

Syrian falafel good enough to open four locations, and a Levantine breakfast from eight on Krupnicza. The everyday end of this cuisine, done right.

How to use this list

Settle the kebab argument yourself: Jamra in Zabłocie for the charcoal Syrian wrap, Soltan on Warszawska for the late-night doner, Tandoor Kebab on Starowiślna for bread baked while you watch, Arabia at the Kleparz market for the shawarma and the free baklava. For a sit-down evening, mezze at Hamsa in Kazimierz. For lunch, hummus at Amamamusi or falafel at Mazaya, each under thirty zloty.

Questions people actually ask

Jamra or Soltan?

Jamra for the charcoal grill and the garlic paste, Soltan for consistency at scale and the late hours. The correct answer is both, on different nights, which is exactly what the city does.

Is there good Middle Eastern breakfast?

Mazaya's Krupnicza flagship serves a Levantine breakfast with shakshuka from 8:00, and Hamsa does weekend breakfasts from 9:00 on Szeroka.

What happened to Sham Falafel?

All signs point to closed: the website domain is gone and its delivery listing shut down in 2024, though stale directory pages still show it. Mazaya and Amamamusi cover the falafel-and-hummus ground it left.
