Best Cafes to Work From a Laptop in Krakow

By Cezary Musiał · Published 8 August 2026 · Hours and details checked August 2026

Krakow is full of beautiful cafes where you absolutely cannot work. Tiny marble tables, one socket hidden behind the fridge, a barista who starts stacking chairs at four. I have been thrown out of enough of them, politely and less politely, to know the difference.

So this list has one rule. Every place on it is somewhere I have actually sat for a few hours with a laptop, ordered a second coffee, and nobody sighed at me. Some cafes in this city limit laptop hours or ban screens at weekends, which is fair enough, their tables, their rules. Those places are not here. Hours below were correct when I checked in August 2026, but Krakow cafes change their minds about Sundays more often than about anything else, so glance at the Google listing before you commit to a cross-town tram ride.

01Bruk Cafe

ul. Krótka 1, Kleparz Mon to Sat 9:00 to 18:00, Sun 9:00 to 14:00 $$

Five minutes on foot from the main station, on the quiet side of Kleparz, and somehow still not overrun. Proper specialty coffee, good matcha if that is your thing, and breakfast served until midday. Sockets within reach of most tables, which in Krakow counts as luxury.

My pick if you have a train to catch later and a deadline before it.

Sockets at tables Breakfast until 12 Near the station Dog-friendly

02I Love Coffee

ul. Kazimierza Wielkiego 86, Krowodrza Check the Google listing for current hours $$

A family-run cafe out in Krowodrza, past the point where tourist maps give up, which is exactly why the tables are free. Coffee done both classic and filter, homemade breakfasts and lunches so you never have to pack up mid-task, plants everywhere and air conditioning that works in August. The kind of place where staff remember your order by the third visit.

Best value work session in this list: residential district prices.

03Bussola Coffee

ul. Józefa Dietla 27, between Old Town and Kazimierz Daily 8:00 to 20:00 $$

On the wide, unglamorous stretch of Dietla that tourists cross but never walk along, which is exactly why there is usually a free table. Long opening hours, calm room, coffee that takes itself seriously without the ceremony. A solid all-day base between two districts.

04Wesoła Immersive

ul. Mikołaja Kopernika 17A, Wesoła Daily 10:00 to 21:00 $$

A new media art center with a cafe attached, in the old hospital quarter that the city is slowly turning into something interesting. Big space, nobody hovering, open until nine in the evening. On a weekday afternoon it might be the quietest room with coffee in central Krakow.

Best for long evening sessions when everything else is closing.

Open until 21 Lots of space Quiet weekdays

05Massolit Books & Café

ul. Felicjanek 4, near Wawel Daily 10:00 to 20:00 $$

An English-language bookshop with tables squeezed between the shelves, run for expats and students long before anyone said the words digital nomad. Not the fastest Wi-Fi in town, but the atmosphere makes up for it: it feels like working in someone's very well-read living room. The cheesecake has its own fan club.

06Fitagain Coffee & Food

ul. Szczepańska 7, Old Town Mon to Sun 8:30 to 18:00 $$

The rare Old Town cafe that actually wants you to work there: a large common table, a bar along the window and more wall sockets than some coworking spaces. Two minutes from the Main Square, so it is also the answer when someone insists on meeting somewhere central and you still have emails to send.

07Urban Coffee

plac Wolnica 12A, Kazimierz Daily 8:00 to 20:00 $$

Kazimierz has plenty of cafes but most of them are built for brunch photos, not for spreadsheets. Urban Coffee, on the calmer plac Wolnica end of the district, is the exception: dependable Wi-Fi, twelve hours open, and far enough from Plac Nowy that you can hear yourself think.

A few honest rules for working in Krakow cafes

Order something every ninety minutes or so. Take calls outside. If the place is filling up for lunch, give up the four-person table without being asked. Do this and Krakow will let you work almost anywhere; skip it and you are the reason the next cafe puts up a no-laptops sign, and I will have to rewrite this article.
