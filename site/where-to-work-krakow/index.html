Best Cafes to Work From a Laptop in Krakow

Published 8 August 2026 · Last verified 8 August 2026 · Every place visited in person

We visited each cafe on this list with a laptop, ran a speed test, counted the sockets and asked the staff about their laptop policy. Places that limit laptop hours or have fewer than four usable seats with power did not make the cut.

01Bruk Cafe

Kleparz Mon to Fri 8:00 to 18:00 $$

A quiet specialty cafe a short walk from the main station, on the Kleparz side of the Old Town. Fast Wi-Fi (120 Mbps down on our test), sockets at most tables and staff that will not give you a look for staying three hours.

Best sockets-per-table ratio on this list.

Wi-Fi 120 Mbps Sockets at tables Dog-friendly Breakfast until 12
