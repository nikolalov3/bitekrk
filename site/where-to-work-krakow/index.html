Best Cafes to Work From a Laptop in Krakow

By Cezary Musiał · Published 8 August 2026 · Hours and details checked August 2026

Krakow is full of beautiful cafes where you absolutely cannot work. Tiny marble tables, one socket hidden behind the fridge, a barista who starts stacking chairs at four. I have been thrown out of enough of them, politely and less politely, to know the difference.

So instead of a list of twenty places I half-remember, here are four I keep coming back to, tested the only way that counts: a full working day in each, laptop open, deadline real. For every one I am telling you exactly what I ordered and whether it was worth it, because you are going to be sitting there for hours and the food matters as much as the sockets.

Hours were correct when I checked in August 2026, but Krakow cafes change their minds about Sundays more often than about anything else, so glance at the Google listing before you commit to a cross-town tram ride.

01Bruk Cafe

ul. Krótka 1, Kleparz Mon to Sat 9:00 to 18:00, Sun 9:00 to 14:00 $$

Five minutes on foot from the main station, on the quiet side of Kleparz, and somehow still not overrun. Proper specialty coffee, a matcha menu longer than in most dedicated matcha bars, breakfast until noon, and sockets within reach of most tables, which in Krakow counts as luxury. I have written half of this website at their window table.

9.5/10
What I ordered
Basque cheesecake + flat white

The cheesecake is the reason to cross town: burnt on top, almost liquid in the middle, impossibly delicate. I planned to eat half and save the rest for the afternoon. The plate did not survive ten minutes.

My pick if you have a train to catch later and a deadline before it.

02I Love Coffee

ul. Kazimierza Wielkiego 86, Krowodrza Check the Google listing for current hours $$

A family-run cafe out in Krowodrza, past the point where tourist maps give up, which is exactly why the tables are free. Coffee done both classic and filter, homemade breakfasts and lunches so you never have to pack up mid-task, plants everywhere and air conditioning that actually works in August. By the third visit the staff remembered my order, which has never once happened to me inside the Planty ring.

9/10
What I ordered
Breakfast plate + cake of the day

Homemade in the literal sense, this tastes like someone's mother is in the kitchen and proud of it. The cake counter changes daily; whatever they cut for you, say yes.

Best value work session on this list: residential district prices.

03Fitagain Coffee & Food

ul. Szczepańska 7, Old Town Mon to Sun 8:30 to 18:00 $$

The rare Old Town cafe that actually wants you to work there: a large common table, a bar along the window and more wall sockets than some coworking spaces. Two minutes from the Main Square, so it is also the answer when someone insists on meeting somewhere central and you still have emails to send. Lunchtime gets lively; mornings are gold.

8.5/10
What I ordered
Breakfast bowl + double espresso

Fresh, generous and not pretending to be anything fancier than it is. Exactly what you want when the laptop battery says three hours and so does your focus.

04Wesoła Immersive

ul. Mikołaja Kopernika 17A, Wesoła Daily 10:00 to 21:00 $$

A new media art center with a cafe attached, in the old hospital quarter that the city is slowly turning into something interesting. Big space, nobody hovering, open until nine in the evening. On a weekday afternoon it might be the quietest room with coffee in central Krakow, and when your eyes need a break from the spreadsheet, there is literal art to stare at.

8/10
What I ordered
Long black, then another one

Solid coffee in a place where the real order is the silence. Two long blacks bought me four uninterrupted evening hours, which in this city is a bargain.

Best for long evening sessions when everything else is closing.

A few honest rules for working in Krakow cafes

Order something every ninety minutes or so. Take calls outside. If the place is filling up for lunch, give up the four-person table without being asked. Do this and Krakow will let you work almost anywhere; skip it and you are the reason the next cafe puts up a no-laptops sign, and I will have to rewrite this article.

Questions people actually ask me

Can you work from a laptop in Krakow cafes at all?

Yes, but choose wisely. Plenty of Old Town cafes limit laptop hours or ban screens at weekends, and nobody puts that on a sign outside. The four places above welcome working guests, that is why they are the whole list.

Which cafe is best if I am near the main station?

Bruk Cafe, without hesitation. Five minutes on foot from Kraków Główny, sockets at most tables, breakfast until noon. I have caught a train with a warm cheesecake in my bag more than once.

How long can I actually sit there?

The unwritten Krakow contract: one order per ninety minutes buys you the seat. Keep to it and a full working day is fine. Break it and you will feel the barista's eyes on your neck, deservedly.

Cafe or coworking, what is cheaper?

A coworking day pass runs 60 to 90 zloty in Krakow. A cafe day is two coffees and a cake, 40 to 60 zloty, and the food is better. Coworking wins only if you need calls, meetings or a monitor.
