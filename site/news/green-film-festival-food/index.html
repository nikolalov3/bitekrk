Green Film Festival: What to Eat on Krakow's River

By Cezary Musiał · Published 13 August 2026 · Details checked August 2026

The ninth BNP Paribas Green Film Festival is running right now on the Bulwary Wisłane, and most guides will tell you about the films. We went for the food.

Every evening until 16 August, a hundred environmental films screen after dusk on a giant outdoor screen at the foot of Wawel Castle. Admission is free, the theme this year is "Air", and the real reason to arrive early is the Strefa Dobrego Smaku: a food zone that takes its eco premise seriously.

The food zone: Strefa Dobrego Smaku

"Good Taste Zone" is the festival's own name for the cluster of food trucks and stands along the Bulwary. The rules are strict: plant-based cuisine, local suppliers, organic baked goods, minimal packaging. Single-use plastic is banned entirely. Drinks come in reusable cups through a deposit system called TAKE CUP! (you pay a small deposit, return the cup, get it back).

Think falafel wraps, grain bowls, vegan burgers, cold-pressed juice, sourdough bread with herbed hummus, and whatever seasonal vegetables the vendors found at the market that morning. Nothing costs more than a standard Krakow lunch, and the atmosphere is closer to a neighborhood picnic than a food festival. Bring a blanket, find a spot on the grass facing the screen, and eat with the castle lit up behind you.

Already on the river: the permanent spots

If the food zone queue is long or you want a full meal before the screening, the Bulwary have their own year-round restaurants within a five-minute walk of the festival site.

Aquarius at Bulwar Czerwieński 81 has a terrace with a direct Wawel view and a menu that runs from brunch plates to evening mains. Wisłany Ogród is a floating platform restaurant on the same stretch, doing traditional and Mediterranean. And for the Instagram moment, the barge restaurants on the Stefan Batory (Pokłady, Nasza Rzeka) serve drinks and small plates while literally sitting on the Vistula.

On the other side of the river, a five-minute walk across the Podgórski bridge, Emalia Zabłocie near the Schindler Factory does excellent breakfast and lunch if you are in the area during the day.

Three more food events this August

Krakow's food calendar does not slow down when the festival ends. Here is what else is happening before September.

Festiwal Pierogów on Mały Rynek (the small square behind the Cloth Hall) runs 12 to 16 August, daily from 10:00. Stalls from across Poland, a pierogi competition, and the shortest walk from the Main Square to a plate of handmade dumplings.

Street Food Polska Festival takes over the parking lot at Galeria Kazimierz on 23 to 24 August with a rotating line-up of food trucks from Warsaw, Wroclaw and local Krakow kitchens.

Gastro Miasto Kraków is the big one: 120-plus vendors return to Bulwar Czerwieński (the same riverbank, just upstream from the Green Film Festival site) on 28 to 30 August. Free admission, live music, silent disco after dark.

Quick reference

When and where is the Green Film Festival?

10 to 16 August 2026, Bulwary Wisłane at the foot of Wawel Castle. Some screenings also run at the square in front of Galeria Krakowska. Films start after dusk (around 20:30 to 21:00 in mid-August). Ninth edition, this year's theme is "Air".

How much does it cost?

Free. All outdoor screenings are open to everyone. The food zone prices are standard street food range, roughly 15 to 35 zloty per dish. Drinks use a reusable cup deposit (TAKE CUP!).

Is there food at the festival itself?

Yes, the Strefa Dobrego Smaku (Good Taste Zone) runs the full duration. Plant-based street food, organic baked goods, local products. No single-use plastic, everything in reusable or compostable packaging.

What about the other August food events?

Festiwal Pierogów: 12 to 16 August, Mały Rynek, daily from 10:00. Street Food Polska: 23 to 24 August, Galeria Kazimierz. Gastro Miasto: 28 to 30 August, Bulwar Czerwieński, 120 vendors, free entry.
