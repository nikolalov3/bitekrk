Krakow Restaurant Week 2026: all 54 restaurants, and the 12 we would book

By Cezary Musiał · Published 12 October 2026 · Details checked October 2026 · How we test

Restaurant Week has been running in Kraków since 7 October and goes on until 25 November. The festival lists 54 restaurants in and around the city, one of them already sold out for the whole edition, and the posted menus are now public, which means the preview stage is over and the real question is where to book. Below is the complete Kraków lineup sorted by district with each kitchen's festival dishes, the twelve tables we would reserve first, and the practical answers people keep asking: price, fee, cancellation, going alone. The rules and booking steps live in the Restaurant Week guide; this is the lineup.

One honesty note. We have not eaten these festival menus yet, since they only exist for seven weeks and the edition started five days ago. The picks below rest on what we know of each kitchen on a normal night and on the menus the restaurants posted, and we say which is which. Festival dishes are quoted from the organiser's listing on 12 October 2026 and can change.

The 12 tables we would book first

Ordered by how confident we are, with the posted festival dishes. Prices are 69.99 zl per person unless stated. Addresses link to our own profile where we have one.

1. Kultova, ul. Bożego Ciała 9, Kazimierz

Voted the best restaurant of a previous edition across all of Poland, and the posted menu is the most ambitious at the standard price: venison tartare with juniper, or skate wing in brown butter with prawns. This is the table to book tonight, before the weekend slots go.

2. Euskadi, ul. Brodzińskiego 4, Podgórze (139 zl)

A Michelin-recommended Basque kitchen across the river, listed in the festival's premium tier. Pluma ibérico with romesco, or squid a la plancha in squid-ink sauce. Twice the standard price and still the cheapest way into this room. More of the district in the Podgórze guide.

3. SOBRE de la MESA, al. 3 Maja 51, Krowodrza

A past festival laureate, Spanish, out by the Błonia meadow. Veal pastrami, or redfish on saffron rice in a wine and butter sauce. One of the few kitchens in the lineup that treats a set menu as a showcase rather than a volume exercise.

4. Halicka Eatery & Bar, ul. Halicka 14A, Kazimierz

Modern Polish on the quiet eastern edge of Kazimierz. Beef tartare followed by veal in demi-glace, or a mushroom zalewajka soup and kopytka dumplings with Femin Blue cheese for the meat-free route. A good first festival booking if you want Polish food that is not folklore.

5. Garnish Food & Wine, ul. Podbrzezie 4, Kazimierz

French, small, by the Tempel Synagogue. Chicken with Robuchon purée, or cod in beurre blanc, then a hazelnut Paris-Brest. The dessert alone justifies the slot.

6. Qrudo Food & Wine, ul. św. Wawrzyńca 19, Kazimierz

Wild boar escalopes with juniper and a tartare on challah, or John Dory with velouté and mussels. The most game-forward menu in the lineup, in a wine bar that knows how to pair it. Compare with our wine bar guide.

7. Zamęt, ul. Stachowicza 15, Zwierzyniec (129 zl)

The autumn opening we flagged in the openings roundup, already in the festival's premium tier: lamb with blackberry, or smoked trout with cider, and a crème brûlée with Polish truffle. A debut at a debut price, so judge it as a tasting, not a bargain.

8. Fala, ul. Konopnickiej 29, Dębniki (99 zl)

A past laureate in the Forum building by the river, in the middle price tier. Duck confit ravioli, or kopytka with forest mushrooms and smoked twaróg. The best vegetarian main in the premium group.

9. Czarnomorka, ul. Kanonicza 15, Old Town

Ukrainian seafood on the prettiest street in the city. An oyster or matjes herring to start, then seafood or salmon with cod. A festival booking that doubles as the easiest way to eat on Kanonicza without a tourist bill.

10. Honō, ul. Zabłocie 19, Zabłocie

The new steakhouse in Zabłocie, also from the openings roundup. Roasted bone marrow and entrecôte with chimichurri, or rostbef tartare and picanha on a skewer. If you wanted an excuse to test the beef, this is a cheap one.

11. Ryoko Ramen, ul. Szewska 5, Old Town

The Asian pick: duck gyoza and shoyu ramen, or yakisoba with smoked tofu, then cannoli with matcha cream. A short walk from the Market Square and a sensible lunch-slot booking. For the city's ramen context, see the ramen guide.

12. Puente, plac Nowy 3, Kazimierz

The only festival restaurant already in our own guides: Spanish and Mexican on plac Nowy. Slow-roasted rib in a blueberry and paprika sauce, or sweet potato stuffed with oyster mushrooms. Hours, rating and what we order on a normal night are on the Puente profile.

Our take

Book the kitchens you would pay full price for. The festival is a discount on a table, not a verdict on a restaurant.

Fifty of the fifty-four menus cost the same, which flattens the field: a hotel restaurant and a Michelin-listed room sit side by side at 69.99 zl. Use that. The lineup is strongest in Kazimierz and weakest in the hotel and chain entries, and the premium tier is only worth it at Euskadi.

Booking in one paragraph

Everything goes through the organiser's site, week.pl, or its app; there is no booking by phone. You pick a restaurant, a date and one of its 90-minute slots, enter two to eight guests, and pay the menu plus 12.99 zl per guest online within 15 minutes. Cancelling is free up to seven days before; after that only the optional Refund Guarantee, 19.99 zl per reservation bought at checkout, gets your money back, and it works until 2:00 on the morning of the visit. Arrive on time, since the restaurant may release the table 15 minutes after the slot starts. Visa cardholders registered in the Visa Benefit programme get 20 or 40 zl off, depending on the card. The full rules, with our take on whether the festival is worth it, are in the Restaurant Week guide. Polish readers have the same lineup and FAQ po polsku.

Questions people actually ask

Which restaurants are in Krakow Restaurant Week 2026?

54 in and around the city. Old Town: Wentzl Cafe Bistro, Nakielny, Butcher Grill, Indus Tandoor, Pod Wawelem, Czarnomorka, Ryoko Ramen, Kuryer, Dragon Tail, Sukiennice, TRIBE Table, Hint. Kazimierz: Kultova, Halicka, Garnish, Qrudo, Chabry, Well Done BBQ, SLAY SPACE, Jodełkowa, Puente, Bella Italia. Podgórze and Zabłocie: Euskadi, Honō, 360°, Sensei Wok, Ciao Bella, Art&Craft Atelier. The rest are in Krowodrza, Grzegórzki, Dębniki, Zwierzyniec, the outer districts and four places outside the city. The full list with dishes is above.

How much is it, and what is the booking fee?

69.99 zl per person at 50 of the 54 restaurants, for three courses plus a Martini-based cocktail or a mocktail. Four premium rooms cost more: Fala 99 zl, Zamęt 129 zl, Euskadi 139 zl, Figatella 139 zl. The booking fee is 12.99 zl per guest, paid online together with the menu.

Do I pay at the restaurant?

No. Menu and fee are paid online within 15 minutes of booking, or the reservation lapses. At the table you pay only for extra drinks, upsells and the tip.

Can I cancel and get my money back?

Free cancellation up to 7 days before the visit, refunded within 7 days. Closer than that, only if you bought the Refund Guarantee, 19.99 zl per reservation, at checkout; it works until 2:00 on the day. Date, time and menu can be changed until 2:00 on the day.

Can I go alone?

Not through the system: reservations are for 2 to 8 guests, and the eighth guest is half price. Ask the restaurant directly if you want a table for one on the festival menu.

Are there vegetarian menus?

Most menus offer two mains and one is often meat-free: roasted vegetables with blue cheese at SADY, pumpkin soup and kopytka at Chabry, kopytka with forest mushrooms at Fala, beetroot gnocchi at TRIBE Table, paneer korma at Indus Tandoor, stuffed tofu at Far East Thai, yakisoba with smoked tofu at Ryoko. Check starters and desserts on the booking page.

Which one is the best?

Kultova on Bożego Ciała 9 if you book one table; Euskadi on Brodzińskiego 4 if you want the splurge at 139 zl. Then SOBRE de la MESA, Halicka, Garnish, Qrudo and Czarnomorka for the best value at 69.99 zl.

When does it end?

25 November 2026. Slots run on a 90-minute grid from 12:00 to 20:45. Weekend evenings go first, and Praziarno was sold out for the whole edition by mid-October.
