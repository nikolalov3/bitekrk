Słońce: The Old Town Gets a New Polish Table

By Cezary Musiał · Published 11 August 2026 · Details checked August 2026

Krakow's Old Town does not often get a genuinely interesting opening, so note the date: days ago, at ul. św. Tomasza 4, a new Polish restaurant called Słońce quietly switched on its lights. The name means "sun", the tagline promises that "the sun rises on the plate and never sets", and behind the poetry sits a very concrete idea: the Sunday Krakow dinner, served every day, without the folksy kitsch that usually comes attached.

The kitchen's opening statement is confidence in slow things. Pierogi are rolled and folded by hand in open view of the room. The broth, a guinea fowl rosół with veal kołduny, simmers for six hours because, as they put it, four is not enough. The menu reads like the canon rewritten by someone who has eaten well abroad and came home anyway: herring with herbed cream and pickled apple, kaszanka on burnt onion with cider sauce, duck pierogi with redcurrant, a whole roast half duck, veal schnitzel with parmesan sauce, and a seasonal page that currently features zucchini flowers stuffed with oscypek cream.

Prices sit where Old Town ambition usually sits: pierogi 34 to 54 zloty, starters around 29 to 41, mains from 49 for the vegan bigos to 108 for the entrecote. Desserts stay under 35, including a house signature called, of course, Słońce: dark chocolate mousse with apricots and pecan praline. There is a garden for walk-ins, indoor tables book online, and the room runs daily from noon to ten.

We will give the kitchen a few weeks to settle and then visit properly for a full review with our usual scoring. For now, the facts are below, and early Google reviews will appear on the card as they come in. If you go before we do, tell me what the pierogi were like: hello@bitekrakow.com.

Context for visitors: św. Tomasza is the quiet-ish lane two minutes north of the Main Square, already home to Milkbar Tomasza from our breakfast guide. If Słońce holds its promise, the street quietly becomes Krakow's best address for Polish food across two price brackets, which is the kind of sentence we will happily verify in person.

The quick facts

Where exactly is it and when is it open?

Ul. św. Tomasza 4, Old Town, two minutes from the Main Square. Daily 12:00 to 22:00. Phone +48 514 112 477.

What does it cost?

Pierogi 34 to 54 zloty, starters 29 to 41, mains 49 to 108, desserts under 35. Old Town prices, but with a six-hour broth behind them.

Can I just walk in?

The garden and a few indoor tables are kept for walk-ins; the rest books online at sloncerestauracja.pl. Groups above 15 choose a set menu.
