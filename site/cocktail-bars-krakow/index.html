Best cocktail bars in Krakow: six rooms worth the night

By Cezary Musiał · Published 4 September 2026 · Details checked September 2026

Krakow's drinking reputation is built on cheap vodka and student bars, and plenty of the Old Town still trades on it. Underneath that, though, is a real cocktail scene: speakeasies behind unmarked doors, bartenders who ask what you like instead of handing you a menu, and drinks that would cost twice as much in Western Europe. This guide is six rooms where the cocktail is the point, split between the Old Town and Kazimierz, ordered by how good the drink is once you weigh the room around it. For the wine version of this night, see our wine bars guide.

01

Mercy Brown

The marquee cocktail bar in the city, on ul. Straszewskiego at the western edge of the Old Town by the Planty. Mercy Brown pairs serious drinks with music and a room that fills with people who came to drink well rather than cheaply, so it earns the highest rating of the six. Dress the part and book at weekends. Close to the Old Town cafes if you want to start earlier.

William Rabbit & Co

The best speakeasy in Krakow, in Kazimierz on ul. Bożego Ciała, entered through doors you have to know about. There is no fixed menu worth reading: you tell the bartender what you like and they build it. It is the drink-and-secret-room experience done properly, not as a gimmick. Part of the Kazimierz night.

Mr. Black

Cocktails under the faded frescoes of a former prayer house on ul. Beera Meiselsa in Kazimierz. How to plan a cocktail night

The two districts are ten minutes apart, so a good night uses both. Start with a classic at Movida or Mr. Black near the Main Square, then walk to Kazimierz for the William Rabbit speakeasy and a last drink under the frescoes at Hevre. Mercy Brown is the one to build an evening around if you only pick one. In summer, Forty Kleparz and its courtyard are worth the short walk north. Cocktails run 28 to 45 PLN, and most rooms fill after ten at weekends, so go early or book. See how it all connects on the food map, or pair it with the wine bars.

Questions people actually ask

Where are the best cocktail bars in Krakow?

Mercy Brown on ul. Straszewskiego 28, off the Planty, is the best and highest rated, a cocktail-and-music bar. For a speakeasy, William Rabbit & Co behind hidden doors in Kazimierz, and Mr. Black up several flights off Szewska. Movida on Mikołajska is the reliable classic.

Does Krakow have speakeasies?

Yes. William Rabbit & Co in Kazimierz, on ul. Bożego Ciała, is a proper speakeasy entered through hidden doors, with bartenders who build a drink to your taste. Mr. Black in the Old Town is a hidden upstairs cocktail room a minute from the Main Square that most people walk past.

How much does a cocktail cost in Krakow?

Roughly 28 to 45 PLN, about 7 to 11 USD. Speakeasies and the more ambitious menus sit at the higher end, and premium spirits cost more. It is still well below what the same drink costs in London or Berlin.

Cocktails in Kazimierz or the Old Town?

Both, ten minutes apart. The Old Town has the marquee and classic rooms, Mercy Brown, Mr. Black and Movida. Kazimierz has the atmosphere, with the William Rabbit speakeasy and cocktails in a former synagogue at Hevre. A good night moves between the two.
