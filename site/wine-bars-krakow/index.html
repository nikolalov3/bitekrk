Best wine bars in Krakow: six rooms worth the bottle

By Cezary Musiał · Published 2 September 2026 · Details checked September 2026 · How we test

Krakow drank beer and vodka for a long time, and wine meant a supermarket bottle with dinner. That has changed fast. The city now has a small, serious wine scene built around two ideas: Polish wine, which barely existed a decade ago and is suddenly worth drinking, and natural wine from small growers across Central and Eastern Europe. This guide is six rooms where the person pouring can tell you what is in the glass and why, from a Polish-focused bar by the market to a Georgian table where amber wine is the point. Ordered by how much we would send a friend there, not by the size of the list.

01

Moti Wine Bar & Shop

The best place in Krakow to drink Polish wine, and the highest rated wine room in the city. Moti sits right by the Stary Kleparz market, a small bar and bottle shop where the focus is growers from Poland and the region that never reach a restaurant list. If you have written off Polish wine, this is the room that changes your mind. Five minutes from the station, and in our Kleparz guide.

Krakó Slow Wines

The natural wine reference point in the city, on ul. Lipowa in Zablocie, a short walk from the Schindler factory and MOCAK. The list is built around small growers working with minimal intervention, mostly from Central and Eastern Europe, and the room is where Krakow's wine people actually drink on their nights off. How to drink wine in Krakow

Two clusters make an easy evening. Kazimierz has NOTO, CiutCiut and Smaki Gruzji within a few minutes of each other, so you can move from a classic glass to natural to Georgian without a tram. Across the river, Vamos and Krakó Slow Wines pair with dinner and the Zablocie art stops. Start Polish at Moti by the market if you only do one, then follow the staff's pours everywhere else. Questions people actually ask

Where can I drink Polish wine in Krakow?

Moti Wine Bar & Shop at Rynek Kleparski 14, by the Stary Kleparz market, is the best place for Polish wine, with growers you will not find on restaurant lists. Poland's wine scene is young but improving fast, and Moti is where to taste why.

Where is the best natural wine in Krakow?

Krakó Slow Wines on ul. Lipowa 6F in Zablocie is the natural wine reference point, focused on small Central and Eastern European growers. In Kazimierz, CiutCiut on ul. Krakowska 6 and NOTO on ul. Beera Meiselsa 14 both carry strong natural selections, and Vamos in Podgorze pairs natural wine with tapas.

How much does a glass of wine cost in a Krakow wine bar?

A glass at a proper wine bar runs roughly 18 to 35 PLN, about 4.50 to 9 USD, depending on the bottle. Natural and small-grower pours sit at the higher end. Bottle shops with a bar, like CiutCiut, let you drink at close to retail price.

What is amber or orange wine, and where can I try it?

Amber wine, often called orange, is white wine made like a red, with the grape skins left in during fermentation, which gives it colour and grip. Georgia has made it this way for thousands of years, so the clearest introduction is Smaki Gruzji in Kazimierz, then the natural lists at Krakó Slow Wines and CiutCiut.
