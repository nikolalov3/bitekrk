Krakow is small enough to walk and varied enough that each district eats its own way. The Old Town has the pierogi classics and the fine-dining tables; Kazimierz turns them into brunch and cocktails; Kleparz keeps the market tradition five minutes from the station; and across the river, Podgorze and Zablocie have become the city's most interesting kitchens. Here is every neighborhood we cover, with its top-rated place and a link to the full guide.

North of the river

The center and around it

Stare Miasto 35 places · guide

The Planty ring: cafes locals actually use, pierogi classics off the Main Square, and the city's fine-dining tables.

Top pick: Karma Coffee

Kazimierz 32 places · guide

The old Jewish quarter, now the bar and brunch heart: Georgian, hummus, tacos, cocktails and serious coffee.

Top pick: Hamsa

Kleparz 11 places · guide

Krakow's oldest market and the specialty coffee around it, five minutes north of the main station.

Top pick: Bruk Cafe

Krowodrza 8 places · guide

Leafy and local, north-west of the center, with coworking, ice cream and quiet neighborhood cafes.

Top pick: Cluster Cowork

Piasek 3 places

Just west of the Old Town, home to the Tytano courtyard, N'Pizza and Syrian falafel on Krupnicza.

Top pick: Mazaya

Wesoła 3 places

Behind the main station, early specialty cafes near the University of Economics.

Top pick: Wesoła Cafe

Stradom guide

The wedge between the Old Town and Kazimierz, with the best fully vegetarian Indian in the city.

Top pick: Bhajan Cafe

Grzegórzki places

East along the river, home to a legendary family ice cream window on Starowiślna.

Top pick: Lody na Starowiślnej

Salwator riverside

Green and quiet at the western edge, the start of the walk up to the Kościuszko Mound.
South of the river

Across the Vistula

Podgórze 10 places · guide

The most interesting side of the river: the best tacos and birria, the Schindler museum, and riverside terraces.

Top pick: Tacoteka

Zabłocie 4 places

Post-industrial and rising: Michelin-listed Korean at Hankki, Syrian charcoal grills and MOCAK.

Top pick: Del Jero

Dębniki place

South of Wawel across the footbridge, a fully vegan sourdough bakery among quiet streets.

Top pick: Zaczyn
Further out

Worth the tram

Nowa Huta 2 places

The planned socialist district far to the east, with one-flavor-a-day ice cream classics and a Renaissance-scale square.

Top pick: Lody Włoskie Mona
