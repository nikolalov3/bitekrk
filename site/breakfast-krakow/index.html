Breakfast in Krakow, For People Who Wake Up Early

By Cezary Musiał · Published 29 July 2026 · Updated 17 October 2026 · Details checked October 2026 · How we test

Krakow is a late city. Most cafes unlock at ten, the famous brunch rooms at nine if you are lucky, and the traveller with an early train or a jet-lagged child is left with hotel buffets and the station bakery. This list is for that person: six independent places that open at seven, eight or nine, checked against their live Google hours in October 2026, with what to order and what it costs. First comes the one we send people to most often, because it covers the morning the rest of the city skips, Sunday. The other five run in order of how early the door opens.

Two places from the original summer list, Handelek and Milkbar Tomasza, closed permanently and were removed. Zaczyn and Kolanko No 6 took their spots. Weekend brunch, a different sport, has its own brunch guide.

01

Bruk Cafe · from 8:00 on weekdays, Sundays too

Eight o'clock on weekdays, nine at the weekend, and breakfast until 1 pm seven days a week: Bruk in Kleparz is the station district's reliable morning, including the Sunday morning slot when most of this list sleeps in and the station district runs on vending machines. Specialty coffee, proper matcha, and the Basque cheesecake that regular readers of this site are tired of hearing about, though not of eating. Five minutes from the station, sockets at the tables, full story in our station coffee guide.

ul. Krótka 1, Kleparz Mon to Fri 8:00 to 18:00, Sat 9:00 to 17:00, Sun 9:00 to 14:00, breakfast until 13:00 $$

The Sunday-morning save of this list. Come at nine, the neighborhood is yours until the church bells finish.

02

Charlotte · from 7:00

The French answer, on plac Szczepański at the edge of the Old Town. Charlotte bakes its own baguettes and croissants and serves the famous basket: bread, homemade preserves, chocolate spread and honey for around 26 zloty, with the square's art nouveau facades thrown in. It opens at seven on weekdays and the weekend queue is a Krakow ritual in itself; go Tuesday at half past seven and have the room nearly to yourself.

plac Szczepański 2, Old Town Mon to Thu 7:00 to midnight, Fri 7:00 to 1:00, Sat 8:00 to 1:00, Sun 8:00 to 22:00 $
8.5/10
What I ordered
The bread basket + café au lait

Warm baguette, four jars, one square of morning sun. Simple arithmetic that has not failed yet.

03

Zaczyn · from 7:00

A sourdough bakery on Kościuszki, a short tram ride west of the Old Town toward Salwator, and the other seven o'clock door in the city. The name means sourdough starter, and the bread is the reason to come: long-fermented loaves, cardamom buns, pastries that crumble the right way. Everything is plant-based, which you will not notice unless told. Take a toast with the seasonal spread and a bun to the window counter, or carry the loaf down to the river. Weekdays from seven, weekends from nine.

ul. Kościuszki 27, west of the Old Town Mon to Fri 7:00 to 18:00, Sat Sun 9:00 to 17:00 $
9/10
What I ordered
Sourdough toast with seasonal bean spread + cardamom bun

The bread alone is worth the trip. Crusty, tangy, properly fermented. The cardamom bun is a 20 zloty reason to skip the croissant.

04

Ósma Rano · from 8:00

The name means eight in the morning, which is both the opening hour and the entire business plan. Opened in 2025 on Podwale by the crew behind Forum Przestrzenie, it runs about ten breakfast sets on bread from the Zaczyn bakery above, plus a build-your-own option, with Garage Coffee Roasters in the grinders. Young, confident and right on the Planty ring, so you can walk off breakfast under the trees.

ul. Podwale 4, Old Town edge Daily 8:00 to 20:00 $$
8.5/10
What I ordered
Build-your-own set on Zaczyn bread

Bread that could carry the plate alone, and a kitchen smart enough to let it.

05

Kolanko No 6 · from 8:00, every day

The Kazimierz institution on Józefa, and the earliest reliable door in the district: eight o'clock seven days a week, Sunday included. Crepes sweet and savoury are the house move, there is a breakfast spread at weekends, and behind the front room sits a vine-covered courtyard that is the best place in Kazimierz to lose a morning. By noon on Saturday every table is taken; at half past eight you choose yours.

ul. Józefa 17, Kazimierz Daily from 8:00, to 22:00 or 23:00 $$
9/10
What I ordered
Savoury crepe, then a sweet one, in the courtyard

The Kazimierz breakfast institution: crepes from eight, a weekend spread, and a vine-covered courtyard that fills by noon. Come early or wait.

06

Ranny Ptaszek · from 8:00

The early bird of Kazimierz, on Augustiańska near the quiet plac Wolnica end of the district. All-morning breakfast with the strongest vegetarian and vegan game on this list, shakshuka included, and a small garden that catches the first sun. Weekdays from eight, weekends from nine, and afterwards you are already standing inside our Kazimierz guide, so make a morning of it.

Breakfast near the Main Square and in the Old Town

The cafes on the Rynek itself charge for the view, and the view is not breakfast. Walk three minutes. Charlotte on plac Szczepański is the closest good morning to the square, from 7:00 on weekdays. Ósma Rano sits on Podwale, on the Planty about six minutes west, from 8:00 every day. Bruk Cafe is about ten minutes north: out through St Florian's Gate, past the Barbican, onto Krótka, which also puts you five minutes from the station. For coffee later in the day, our Old Town cafes guide picks up where this one stops.

The early riser's cheat sheet

Off the train: Bruk in Kleparz, five minutes from the platforms, any day of the week. Seven o'clock with a view: Charlotte on plac Szczepański. Seven o'clock with the best bread: Zaczyn on Kościuszki. Sunday at eight in Kazimierz: Kolanko No 6. Vegetarian company: Ranny Ptaszek. And the newest kitchen on the list, Ósma Rano, for when you want the Planty trees with your second coffee. If you need a seat with a socket after breakfast, the laptop guide takes over from here.

Questions people actually ask me

What is the best breakfast in Krakow?

Bruk Cafe by the station for coffee and a full plate, breakfast until 13:00 every day. Charlotte on plac Szczepański for a French bread basket at 7:00. Kolanko No 6 in Kazimierz for crepes in a courtyard from 8:00. Zaczyn on Kościuszki for the best bread.

Breakfast near the Main Square?

Charlotte on plac Szczepański, three minutes away, from 7:00 on weekdays. Ósma Rano on Podwale, six minutes west, from 8:00. Bruk Cafe, ten minutes north past the Barbican.

Where can I eat before 8 in the morning?

Charlotte from 7:00 on plac Szczepański, used to travellers with luggage, and Zaczyn from 7:00 on Kościuszki if bread is the point. Everything else on this list opens at 8:00 or 9:00.

Sunday breakfast: what is actually open early?

Kolanko No 6 and Charlotte from 8:00, Bruk Cafe and Ranny Ptaszek from 9:00. Arrive before ten or accept the queue as part of the experience.

Breakfast near the main station?

Bruk Cafe at ul. Krótka 1, five minutes on foot from Kraków Główny, breakfast until 1 pm seven days a week. The rest of the station district is covered in the station coffee guide.

How much should breakfast cost?

Twenty to forty zloty for a full plate at every place on this list, under thirty at the two bakeries. If a central Krakow breakfast costs more than fifty, you are paying for the location twice.

Where do I get a traditional Polish breakfast?

The sit-down cafes on this list serve the Krakow market-produce tradition: bread with cold cuts, cheese and eggs. It is how the city has eaten its mornings for generations.
