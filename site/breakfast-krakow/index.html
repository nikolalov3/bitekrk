Breakfast in Krakow, For People Who Wake Up Early

By Cezary Musiał · Published 29 July 2026 · Hours checked August 2026

02

Charlotte · from 7:00

The French answer, on plac Szczepański at the edge of the Old Town. Charlotte bakes its own baguettes and croissants and serves the famous basket: bread, homemade preserves, chocolate spread and honey for around 26 zloty, with the square's art nouveau facades thrown in. It opens at seven on weekdays and the weekend queue is a Krakow ritual in itself; go Tuesday at half past seven and have the room nearly to yourself.

plac Szczepański 2, Old Town Mon to Fri from 7:00, Sat Sun from 8:00 $

03

Bruk Cafe · from 9:00, Sundays too

Nine o'clock is not heroic, but consistency is: Bruk in Kleparz serves breakfast until noon seven days a week, including the Sunday morning slot when most of this list sleeps in and the station district runs on vending machines. Specialty coffee, proper matcha, and the Basque cheesecake that regular readers of this site are tired of hearing about, though not of eating. Five minutes from the station, sockets at the tables, full story in our station coffee guide.

04

Ósma Rano · from 8:00

The name means eight in the morning, which is both the opening hour and the entire business plan. Opened in 2025 on Podwale by the crew behind Forum Przestrzenie, it runs about ten breakfast sets on bread from the Zaczyn bakery, plus a build-your-own option, with Garage Coffee Roasters in the grinders. Young, confident and right on the Planty ring, so you can walk off breakfast under the trees.

Ranny Ptaszek · from 8:00

The early bird of Kazimierz, on Augustiańska near the quiet plac Wolnica end of the district. All-morning breakfast with the strongest vegetarian and vegan game on this list, shakshuka included, and a small garden that catches the first sun. Weekdays from eight, weekends from nine, and afterwards you are already standing inside our Kazimierz guide, so make a morning of it.

The early riser's cheat sheet

Seven o'clock with a view: Charlotte on plac Szczepański. Sunday morning: Bruk from nine, when almost nothing else in the station district moves. Vegetarian company: Ranny Ptaszek in Kazimierz. And the newest kitchen on the list, Ósma Rano, for when you want the Planty trees with your second coffee.

Questions people actually ask me

Where can I eat before 8 in the morning?

Charlotte from 7:00, a short walk from the Old Town and used to travelers with luggage. Most other independent spots open from 8:00.

Sunday breakfast: what is actually open early?

Bruk Cafe from 9:00 in Kleparz, Charlotte from 8:00, and Ranny Ptaszek from 9:00. Arrive before ten or accept the queue as part of the experience.

How much should breakfast cost?

Twenty to forty zloty for a full plate at every place on this list. If a central Krakow breakfast costs more than fifty, you are paying for the location twice.

Where do I get a traditional Polish breakfast?

The sit-down cafes on this list serve the Krakow market-produce tradition: bread with cold cuts, cheese and eggs. It is how the city has eaten its mornings for generations.
