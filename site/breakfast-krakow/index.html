Breakfast in Krakow, For People Who Wake Up Early

By Cezary Musiał · Published 29 July 2026 · Hours checked August 2026

Krakow is a late-breakfast town pretending otherwise. Half the places that advertise "breakfast all day" do not unlock their doors until ten, which is lunch with extra steps. This list is for the other appetite: jet lag, early trains, small children, or the simple conviction that a city belongs to whoever is up first.

Every place below opens at nine at the latest, most much earlier, and each entry states the exact weekday and weekend hours, because that is the whole point of the article. Prices run 20 to 40 zloty for a proper plate.

01

Handelek · from 6:30

The earliest serious breakfast in Krakow changed its address in 2026: Handelek left its legendary corner on św. Filipa and now feeds the city from Ślusarska 9 in Zabłocie, across the river among the district's post-industrial lofts. The essentials survived the move intact: the traditional krakowskie śniadanie, heritage-recipe sandwiches on bread that means it, market produce, budget prices. At half past six you share the room with Zabłocie's early shift, and by nine the queue looks like the old days.

02

Charlotte · from 7:00

The French answer, on plac Szczepański at the edge of the Old Town. Charlotte bakes its own baguettes and croissants and serves the famous basket: bread, homemade preserves, chocolate spread and honey for around 26 zloty, with the square's art nouveau facades thrown in. It opens at seven on weekdays and the weekend queue is a Krakow ritual in itself; go Tuesday at half past seven and have the room nearly to yourself.

03

Bruk Cafe · from 9:00, Sundays too

Nine o'clock is not heroic, but consistency is: Bruk in Kleparz serves breakfast until noon seven days a week, including the Sunday morning slot when most of this list sleeps in and the station district runs on vending machines. Specialty coffee, proper matcha, and the Basque cheesecake that regular readers of this site are tired of hearing about, though not of eating. Five minutes from the station, sockets at the tables, full story in our station coffee guide.

04

Ósma Rano · from 8:00

The name means eight in the morning, which is both the opening hour and the entire business plan. Opened in 2025 on Podwale by the crew behind Forum Przestrzenie, it runs about ten breakfast sets on bread from the Zaczyn bakery, plus a build-your-own option, with Garage Coffee Roasters in the grinders. Young, confident and right on the Planty ring, so you can walk off breakfast under the trees.

05

Milkbar Tomasza · from 8:00

The milk bar, modernized without being embalmed. On św. Tomasza in the Old Town, it serves the big scrambled-egg breakfasts, pancakes and pierogi of the bar mleczny tradition at prices that still respect the tradition, from eight every day except Monday, when it rests. Locals and tourists share the tables in unusual harmony, mostly because everyone is too busy eating.

Ranny Ptaszek · from 8:00

The early bird of Kazimierz, on Augustiańska near the quiet plac Wolnica end of the district. All-morning breakfast with the strongest vegetarian and vegan game on this list, shakshuka included, and a small garden that catches the first sun. Weekdays from eight, weekends from nine, and afterwards you are already standing inside our Kazimierz guide, so make a morning of it.

The early riser's cheat sheet

Weekday at dawn: Handelek at 6:30, now in Zabłocie. Seven o'clock with a view: Charlotte on plac Szczepański. Sunday morning: Bruk from nine, when almost nothing else in the station district moves. Vegetarian company: Ranny Ptaszek in Kazimierz. Biggest plate for the smallest bill: Milkbar Tomasza, any day but Monday. And the newest kitchen on the list, Ósma Rano, for when you want the Planty trees with your second coffee.

Questions people actually ask me

Where can I eat before 8 in the morning?

Handelek from 6:30 on weekdays, Charlotte from 7:00. Both are within ten minutes of the main station and the Old Town respectively, and both are used to travelers with luggage.

Sunday breakfast: what is actually open early?

Bruk Cafe from 9:00 in Kleparz, Charlotte from 8:00, Milkbar Tomasza and Ranny Ptaszek from 9:00. Arrive before ten or accept the queue as part of the experience.

How much should breakfast cost?

Twenty to forty zloty for a full plate at every place on this list. If a central Krakow breakfast costs more than fifty, you are paying for the location twice.

Where do I get a traditional Polish breakfast?

Handelek for the Krakow market-produce tradition, Milkbar Tomasza for the milk-bar version. Between the two of them you have the whole story of how this city eats its mornings.
