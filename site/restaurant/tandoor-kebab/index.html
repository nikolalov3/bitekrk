Tandoor Kebab

Opened autumn 2025 and bakes each flatbread to order in a real tandoor, reportedly the first kebab in Krakow to do so.

Our take

9/10 · BITEKRAKOW SCORE
Halal, and the only kebab in the city baking its bread to order in a tandoor. The chicken in fresh bread resets what you expect from a wrap.
