Dangol

A basement Korean dining room under the owners' own grocery, with shared K-hot pots and semi-private rooms for groups.

Our take

8.5/10 · BITEKRAKOW SCORE
Shared hot pots in a basement under a real Korean grocery, with private mini-rooms for groups. The most fun way to eat Korean in this city, and the shopping is downstairs-upstairs.
