COPPA

A specialty coffee cafe on Mazowiecka that specializes in tiramisu. The dessert leads, with a homemade kogel-mogel richness, and the coffee is taken seriously. Open seven days a week.

Our take

9/10 · BITEKRAKOW SCORE
The fullest autumn card in the city, built on house syrups rather than bought ones, with a plum tiramisu to finish.

From Autumn in Krakow, by the cup and by the plate

NEW OPENING
Early taste, not a full review yet. The tiramisu is the reason to come: a genuine kogel-mogel richness under the cocoa, the homemade kind, and it works. The coffee and the room we will judge on a proper sit-down.

Menu

From COPPA's own menu, translated by us. Checked 18 October 2026. Current menu on their site.

Tiramisu, always on

  • Classic tiramisu Tiramisu klasyczne

    Where COPPA started, inspired by tiramisu from travels in Italy. No alcohol.

  • Pistachio tiramisu Tiramisu pistacjowe

    Pistachio from the first spoon to the last: intensely nutty and silky. No alcohol.

Tiramisu, seasonal

  • Earl Grey tiramisu Tiramisu Earl Grey

    Bergamot with citrus and floral notes. No coffee, no alcohol.

  • Hojicha tiramisu Tiramisu hojicha

    Roasted Japanese green tea: nuts, caramel, a little smoke, umami, with a hojicha ganache. No coffee, no alcohol.

  • Limoncello tiramisu Tiramisu limoncello

    An alcohol-free take on limoncello, with citrus peel oils and an aromatic dusting. No coffee, no alcohol.

  • White peach and lemon thyme tiramisu Tiramisu białe brzoskwinie i tymianek cytrynowy

    Sweet white peach and the herbal freshness of lemon thyme. Seasonal.

  • Hazelnut praline tiramisu Tiramisu pralina z orzecha laskowego

    Caramel hazelnut praline with a crunchy top on a silky cream. Seasonal.

  • Matcha and raspberry tiramisu Tiramisu matcha i malina

    Green, umami matcha with fruity raspberry. No coffee, no alcohol.

  • Plum and crumble tiramisu Tiramisu śliwka z kruszonką

    Ripe plums and crunchy crumble on a silky cream. The taste of autumn.

Sandwiches

  • Salami piccante sandwich Kanapka salami piccante

    Ciabatta, salami piccante, ricotta, chilli honey, rocket and red onion. Hot and sweet at once.

  • Vegetarian sandwich Kanapka wege

    Ciabatta, olives, sun-dried tomatoes, stracciatella, rocket and balsamic olive oil. Salty, creamy, meat-free.

  • Mortadella sandwich Kanapka z mortadelą

    Ciabatta, mortadella, stracciatella, mascarpone, lemon, pistachio and rocket. An Italian classic with lemon freshness.

Autumn coffees

  • Iced Banana Bread

    Banana bread as an iced coffee: ripe banana, cinnamon, milk and espresso.

  • Iced Apple Pie Latte

    Baked autumn apple with espresso and milk over ice, creamy and gently sweet, like a home-made apple pie.

  • Pumpkin Spice

    House syrup of roasted pumpkin and warm spices with a note of orange. Creamy, deep and balanced in sweetness.

  • Banana Bread Latte

    Banana bread turned into a creamy hot coffee, to the recipe of barista Karola.

Autumn drinks

  • White Vanilla Matcha

    Matcha with house vanilla syrup enriched with tonka and white chocolate. Mild, creamy, dessert-like.

  • Berry Cocoa

    House cocoa with raspberry purée: milk chocolate sweetness cut with a little raspberry acidity.

  • Dark Chocolate Mocha

    Double espresso with dark cocoa and milk. Intensely chocolatey and deep.

  • Oat Cinnamon Hojicha

    Roasted Japanese tea with oat milk and warm spices. Lightly sweet, nutty and warming.

Coffee

House espresso from Roastains, a second grinder with rotating fruit-forward beans (the "funky flat white") and guest roasters such as Gringo Nordic. Classic, lactose-free, oat or pea milk at no extra charge.
