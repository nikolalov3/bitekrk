Krakow Restaurant Week 2026: how it works and how to book

By Cezary Musiał · Published 6 October 2026 · Details checked October 2026 · How we test

Krakow Restaurant Week is the biggest dining event in the city's calendar. For seven weeks, more than fifty restaurants each cook a special three-course menu with a cocktail for one fixed price, and you book the ones you want online. This is the preview, written before it starts: the numbers, the booking steps and an honest take on whether it is worth your evening. Not a review, since nobody has eaten the festival menus yet.

How to book, in four steps

  1. Open the official list

    All participating restaurants, their festival menus and the free time slots live on the RestaurantClub site. There is no booking by phone or at the door.

  2. Pick a restaurant and a slot

    Read the three-course menu first, since it is fixed and smaller than the regular carte. The best-reviewed rooms and Friday and Saturday evenings go first.

  3. Pay the 9.99 zl fee to confirm

    Charged per person at checkout. It holds the table and is the reason the festival has so few no-shows. Visa cardholders usually see a small extra discount here.

  4. Pay 70 zl at the table

    The meal itself is settled at the restaurant. Extra drinks beyond the included cocktail, and the tip, are on top.

Is it worth it

Our take

Yes, with one rule: go for the restaurant, not the discount.

At 70 zl for three courses and a cocktail, the value is real, especially at the mid-range and upper rooms that make up most of the lineup. The trade-off is a fixed menu that shows you a taste of a kitchen, not its full range. Treat it as a low-risk way to finally try the place you have been meaning to book, not as a way to eat cheaply at the fanciest table in town.

Past Krakow editions have run from steakhouses like Ed Red and Mucca to modern Polish and Italian tables, and Kultova was voted the festival's best restaurant in Poland in a recent round. The current lineup changes each edition, so check the official list rather than last year's.

Questions people actually ask

When is Krakow Restaurant Week 2026?

The autumn 2026 edition runs from 7 October to 25 November 2026. The festival happens twice a year, in spring and autumn, and each edition lasts a little over a month.

How much does it cost?

The set menu is 70 zl per person, about 17 USD, for three courses and a cocktail, plus a 9.99 zl booking fee paid online when you reserve. Drinks beyond the cocktail and the tip are extra.

How do you book?

Online only, through the official RestaurantClub site. Choose a restaurant and a time slot, pay the 9.99 zl fee to confirm, and pay the 70 zl at the restaurant. Popular restaurants and weekend slots fill early, so book ahead.

Is it worth it?

For three courses and a cocktail at a mid-range or upper restaurant, 70 zl is good value and a low-risk way to try a new place. The festival menu is fixed and smaller than the full carte, and the best restaurants book out fast. Go for the restaurant, not the discount.
