Krakow Restaurant Week 2026: how it works and how to book

By Cezary Musiał · Published 6 October 2026 · Updated 12 October 2026 · Details checked October 2026 · How we test

Krakow Restaurant Week is the biggest dining event in the city's calendar. For seven weeks, 54 restaurants each cook a special three-course menu with a cocktail for one fixed price, and you book the ones you want online. The edition is now running, the menus are public and one restaurant is already sold out. This page has the rules as they stand in the organiser's autumn 2026 regulations, the booking steps, and an honest take on whether it is worth your evening. How to book, in four steps

  1. Open the official Kraków list

    All participating restaurants, their festival menus and the free slots live on the organiser's site, week.pl, and in the Week app. There is no booking by phone or at the door.

  2. Pick a restaurant, a date and a slot

    Slots run on a 90-minute grid: 12:00, 13:45, 15:30, 17:15, 19:00 and 20:45, though not every restaurant opens every one. Read the three-course menu first, since it is fixed and smaller than the regular carte. Weekend evenings at the best-reviewed rooms go first.

  3. Pay online, for 2 to 8 guests

    The menu plus 12.99 zl per guest, by card or bank transfer, within 15 minutes of placing the booking or it lapses. At checkout you can add the Refund Guarantee for 19.99 zl per reservation, the only way to get your money back if you cancel later than 7 days before; it works until 2:00 on the morning of the visit. Visa cardholders registered in Visa Benefit get 20 or 40 zl off.

  4. Turn up on time

    Bring the confirmation on your phone. The restaurant may release the table 15 minutes after the slot starts. Extra drinks beyond the included cocktail, any upsells, and the tip are paid at the table; the menu itself is already settled.

Is it worth it

Our take

Yes, with one rule: go for the restaurant, not the discount.

At 69.99 zl for three courses and a cocktail, plus the 12.99 zl fee, the value is real, especially at the mid-range and upper rooms that make up most of the lineup. The trade-off is a fixed menu that shows you a taste of a kitchen, not its full range. Fifty of the fifty-four restaurants cost exactly the same, which puts a hotel dining room and a Michelin-recommended kitchen side by side at one price. Treat the festival as a low-risk way to finally try the place you have been meaning to book, not as a way to eat cheaply at the fanciest table in town.

Questions people actually ask

When is Krakow Restaurant Week 2026?

7 October to 25 November 2026, so 25 November is the last day. The festival runs twice a year, spring and autumn, about seven weeks each. Slots are 90 minutes on a grid from 12:00 to 20:45.

How much does it cost?

69.99 zl per person at 50 of the 54 restaurants, for three courses plus a Martini-based cocktail or a mocktail. Four premium rooms cost 99 to 139 zl: Fala, Zamęt, Euskadi, Figatella. The booking fee is 12.99 zl per guest, paid online with the menu, so dinner for two at a standard restaurant is 165.96 zl before extra drinks and the tip.

How do you book?

Online only, on week.pl or in the Week app. Choose a restaurant, a date and a slot, enter 2 to 8 guests, and pay the menu plus the fee online within 15 minutes. No phone bookings, no walk-ins on the festival menu.

Do I pay at the restaurant?

No. Menu and fee are paid online at booking. At the table you pay only for extra drinks, upsells and the tip. Arrive on time: the restaurant may release the table 15 minutes after the slot starts.

Can I cancel?

Free up to 7 days before the visit, refunded within 7 days. Closer than that, only with the Refund Guarantee, 19.99 zl per reservation bought at checkout, which works until 2:00 on the day. Date, time and menu can be changed until 2:00 on the day.

Can I go alone?

Not through the system: reservations are for 2 to 8 guests, with the eighth at half price. Ask the restaurant directly for a table for one.

Which restaurants take part?

54 in and around Kraków: Kultova, Halicka, Garnish, Qrudo and Puente in Kazimierz; Czarnomorka, Ryoko Ramen, Butcher Grill, Wentzl Cafe Bistro, Sukiennice and TRIBE Table in the Old Town; Euskadi, Honō and 360° across the river; SOBRE de la MESA in Krowodrza; Fala in Dębniki; Zamęt in Zwierzyniec, and more. The full list with dishes is in the lineup article.

Is it worth it?

Yes, if you book kitchens you would pay full price for. Three courses and a cocktail at a serious restaurant for 69.99 zl plus the fee is good value and a low-risk way to try somewhere new. The menu is fixed and short, and almost every restaurant costs the same, so choose the kitchen, not the discount.
