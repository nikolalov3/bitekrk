Krakow Restaurant Week 2026: how it works and how to book

By Cezary Musiał · Published 6 October 2026 · Details checked October 2026 · How we test

Krakow Restaurant Week is the biggest dining event in the city's calendar, and the autumn 2026 edition runs from 7 October to 25 November 2026. The idea is simple: more than fifty restaurants each cook a special three-course menu with a cocktail for one fixed price, and you book the ones you want online. This is the preview, written before it starts, so you can plan the week rather than scramble for a table. We will not pretend to have eaten the festival menus yet; this is the how and the how-much, not a review.

The deal, in numbers

The set menu is 70 zl per person, roughly 17 USD, for three courses and a cocktail. On top of that there is a 9.99 zl booking fee per person, paid online when you reserve, which holds the table. Drinks beyond the included cocktail and the tip are extra. For a three-course dinner with a drink at a proper restaurant, that is a good price, and it is the cheapest way to sit down somewhere you might never otherwise book.

How to book

Booking is online only, through the official RestaurantClub site, where the full list of participating restaurants, their festival menus and the available time slots all live. You pick a restaurant and a slot, pay the 9.99 zl fee to confirm, and settle the 70 zl at the table. The system is reservation-first on purpose, which cuts no-shows and food waste. Two practical notes: the best-reviewed restaurants and Friday and Saturday evenings go first, so book early, and Visa cardholders usually get a small extra discount at checkout.

Is it worth it, honestly

Yes, with one rule: go for the restaurant, not the discount. At 70 zl for three courses and a cocktail, the value is real, especially at the mid-range and upper places that make up most of the lineup. The trade-off is that the festival menu is fixed and usually smaller than the full carte, so it is a taste of a kitchen rather than its full range. Treat it as a low-risk way to finally try the restaurant you have been meaning to, not a way to eat cheaply at the fanciest room in town. Past Krakow editions have run from steakhouses like Ed Red and Mucca to modern Polish and Italian tables, with Kultova voted the festival's best in Poland in a recent round; the current list is on the official site.

Questions people actually ask

When is Krakow Restaurant Week 2026?

The autumn 2026 edition runs from 7 October to 25 November 2026. The festival happens twice a year, in spring and autumn, and each edition lasts a little over a month.

How much does it cost?

The set menu is 70 zl per person, about 17 USD, for three courses and a cocktail, plus a 9.99 zl booking fee paid online when you reserve. Drinks beyond the cocktail and the tip are extra.

How do you book?

Online only, through the official RestaurantClub site. Choose a restaurant and a time slot, pay the 9.99 zl fee to confirm, and pay the 70 zl at the restaurant. Popular restaurants and weekend slots fill early, so book ahead.

Is it worth it?

For three courses and a cocktail at a mid-range or upper restaurant, 70 zl is good value and a low-risk way to try a new place. The festival menu is fixed and smaller than the full carte, and the best restaurants book out fast. Go for the restaurant, not the discount.
