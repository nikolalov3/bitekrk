The Best Italian in Krakow, From Pizza to Truffle Pasta

By Cezary Musiał · Published 19 August 2026 · Addresses and prices checked August 2026

Krakow took a while to take Italian food seriously, and then it took it very seriously indeed. The turning point was one small pizzeria in Kazimierz proving that a proper Neapolitan pie, dough left to proof for hours and blistered in a wood oven, could be made here as well as anywhere. Now the city has a genuine spread: benchmark pizza, homemade pasta in medieval cellars, truffle on the prettiest street in town, and one seafood room that plays in a different league.

A note on Michelin, because people ask. No dedicated Italian restaurant in Krakow holds a Michelin distinction right now. The city's starred and recommended tables are Polish, Nordic or seafood, so everything below is ranked the honest way, on the cooking and the room, not on a red guide. Six places, from a 40 zloty pizza to a lobster dinner, roughly best-to-different rather than a strict ladder.

Kazimierz · pizza

Nolio

Start with the one that started it. Nolio is widely credited with kicking off Krakow's whole Neapolitan-pizza wave, and it still sets the standard. The dough proofs for around eight hours and spends roughly a minute in the wood-burning oven, which is exactly why the cornicione comes out puffed, charred and light. The entrance porch is tiny, so weekend evenings mean a queue. There is a second outpost, Nolio Pop, on nearby Miodowa if the main room is full.

ul. Krakowska 27, Kazimierz Tue to Sun, dinner focused, closed Mondays $$
9.5/10
What to order
Margherita DOP + the melanzane pizza

San Marzano passata, cooked buffalo mozzarella, a cornicione that stays airy under the char. The place that taught Krakow what a Neapolitan pizza is supposed to feel like.

Piasek · pizza

N'Pizza

The best value on this list and, on the right night, the best pizza too. N'Pizza sits inside the Tytano courtyard on the Old Town's western edge, a bright modern room with full-length windows and an open pizza station. The dough is well hydrated and bouncy, scorched about a minute in the wood-fired oven with carefully sourced Italian ingredients. Watch the specials board for rotating seasonal pies, and use it as the meal before a night out in Tytano's bars.

ul. Rajska 3, entrance from Dolnych Młynów, Piasek Lunch and dinner $
9/10
What to order
Buffalo-mozzarella Margherita + whatever is on the specials board

Top-tier Neapolitan dough at the friendliest prices in town, in the middle of the best courtyard for a night out. Hard to do better for the money.

Old Town · trattoria

Trattoria Soprano

Two minutes from the Rynek, on a street where most kitchens coast on tourist footfall, Soprano quietly does the opposite. A rustic, intimate room with a real wood-fired oven and pasta made in house, it is the reliable all-rounder: honest portions, a proper carbonara, gnocchi that taste handmade because they are. The review count is one of the highest of any Italian in the city, which for a back-street trattoria tells you the locals kept the secret badly.

ul. Świętej Anny 7, Old Town Lunch and dinner $$
8.5/10
What to order
Mushroom gnocchi + spaghetti with mussels

Homemade pasta and a wood-fired oven two minutes from the Main Square, run like a neighborhood place rather than a tourist trap. Book on weekend evenings.

Old Town · atmosphere

Trattoria La Campana

On Kanonicza, arguably the prettiest street in Krakow, running down to Wawel, La Campana hides an ivy-draped garden behind a medieval townhouse. In summer that cobbled patio is the reason to come, the best outdoor dining room in the Old Town. The kitchen leans truffle: ravioli con tartufo, tagliatelle with black truffle, a clean tuna carpaccio to start. It is the special-occasion Italian, the one you book for a date and let the setting do half the work.

ul. Kanonicza 7, Old Town Lunch and dinner, garden in summer $$$
9/10
What to order
Ravioli con tartufo + a table in the garden

Truffle pasta under the ivy on Krakow's loveliest street. The food is genuinely good, the setting does the rest. Reserve the garden well ahead in summer.

Old Town · classic

Restauracja Corleone

The Godfather name and the themed decor read touristy, and plenty of locals walk past on principle. They are missing out. Corleone is a long-established, slightly formal traditional Italian just off the Main Square, with classic pastas, veal and beef mains, a solid wine list and, crucially, a kitchen that stays open to midnight every day. When it is eleven at night and you want a proper plate of pasta rather than a kebab, this is the answer.

How to use this list

Pizza night, cross into Kazimierz for Nolio, or stay central and cheap at N'Pizza in the Tytano courtyard. A date or a birthday, book the garden at La Campana. A reliable sit-down dinner two steps from the Rynek, Trattoria Soprano. And when it is late and everything else has closed, Corleone keeps the pasta coming to midnight.

Two more worth noting if you have crossed the river: Pizzeria 00 and Olio in Podgorze are the district's modern pizza standouts, Olio with a riverside terrace. And if you want the closest thing to a Michelin table with Italian leanings, Farina on Świętego Marka is Michelin-recommended, though it cooks seafood over salt and herbs rather than a classic Italian menu.

Questions people actually ask

Where is the best pizza in Krakow?

Nolio on Krakowska in Kazimierz is the consensus best Neapolitan, the place credited with starting the city's pizza wave. N'Pizza in the Tytano courtyard is the best value and close behind. Both blister the dough in a wood oven in about a minute.

How much should I budget for Italian in Krakow?

A pizza and a drink is around 40 to 70 zloty. Trattoria pasta mains run 35 to 55 zloty. Fine dining at La Campana climbs to 60 to 120 zloty for mains. Italian here spans real budget to genuine splurge.

Which Italian has the best atmosphere?

Trattoria La Campana, for the ivy garden on Kanonicza in summer, the prettiest outdoor dining room in the Old Town. Book the garden ahead.

Can I get good Italian late at night?

Restauracja Corleone runs its kitchen to midnight every day, just off the Main Square. It is the reliable late option when the wood-fired pizzerias, which are more dinner-focused, have already closed.
